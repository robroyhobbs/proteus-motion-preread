Core thesis

Proteus does not need broader marketing first. It needs a clearer funnel.

My bias: start narrow, lead with proof, and make the sales conversation easier before trying to scale attention.

Attract

Lead with a sharper point of view for one buyer.

  • Choose one wedge
  • Clarify headline and promise
  • Publish founder and customer proof

Convert

Turn the report, outcomes, and testimonials into a clear proof path.

  • Feature the sample report
  • Use before/after or outcome proof
  • Create one focused page with one CTA

Close

Give sales a cleaner commercial story so calls start from trust, not explanation.

  • Operator ROI narrative
  • Proof snippets for follow-up
  • Clear handoff from interest to meeting
Why this is workable now

The foundation is already there

1. Brand and audience momentum

Caley helped build real top-of-funnel traction: Instagram growth from roughly 1.2k to 10k organically in 13 months, now over 20k, plus TikTok past 10k.

2. Owned community and repeatable formats

Proteus already has more than social reach. There is a community hub, repeatable content formats, and an ambassador program — all signs of real GTM infrastructure.

3. The next opportunity

The next win is not starting content over. It is turning existing attention and proof into a cleaner conversion path, stronger proof packaging, and better sales enablement.

0-30 day plan

What I would do first

Days 0-5 · choose the wedge and map the funnel

  • Choose the primary wedge
  • Review the current site, sales flow, and proof assets
  • Map the path from first touch to booked conversation
  • Find where prospects get confused or stall

Days 6-10 · package one flagship proof asset

  • Build one proof-led story around a real outcome
  • Use the report, quote, and business case together
  • Create proof snippets for email, social, and follow-up
  • Turn one source into reusable funnel assets

Days 11-20 · tighten the conversion path

  • Ship one focused landing path for the wedge
  • Clarify the CTA
  • Use the report and proof assets to pre-sell the conversation
  • Align sales follow-up with the same segment story and proof stack

Days 21-30 · launch, learn, and sharpen

  • Publish a short founder-led sequence for the same wedge
  • Track which claims and proof points earn the strongest response
  • Refine the page, proof pack, and follow-up based on live feedback
  • Decide whether to deepen the first wedge or expand to the next one
What this should produce

What should exist by day 30

Core deliverables

  • One clear wedge-specific message
  • One flagship proof asset
  • One tighter landing path with a clear CTA
  • One short founder-led content sequence
  • One short set of website and funnel recommendations

Metrics I would care about early

  • More qualified conversations from the target wedge
  • Higher confidence in sales calls because the story is clearer sooner
  • Better reuse of proof across site, email, and content
  • Clearer signal on which wedge and which promise convert best
What I need from the Proteus team

The inputs that would let me move fast

Commercial and funnel inputs

  • Which wedge matters most right now
  • Current website, demo, and sales follow-up flow
  • Top objections and where deals tend to slow down
  • What success means over the next quarter

Proof inputs

  • Sample reports and assessment outputs
  • Best customer stories, testimonials, and clips
  • Any outcome data tied to training, rehab, or operator ROI
  • Founder interviews or source material worth atomizing

Working with Caley: I would treat this as an extension of the foundation she already built — not a restart. Week one should clarify the wedge, priorities, review cadence, and who owns strategy, asset creation, distribution, and follow-through so the work compounds instead of colliding.
