Core thesis

Proteus does not need broader marketing first. It needs a tighter funnel.

My bias: start narrow, package proof aggressively, and give the sales conversation a stronger runway before anyone books a demo.

Attract

Lead with a sharper point of view for one buyer, not a general story for everyone.

  • Choose one wedge
  • Clarify headline and promise
  • Publish founder and customer proof

Convert

Make the product easier to believe by turning the report, outcomes, and testimonial evidence into a clear proof path.

  • Feature the sample report
  • Use before/after or outcome proof
  • Create one focused page with one CTA

Close

Give sales a cleaner commercial story so every conversation starts from trust instead of explanation.

  • Operator ROI narrative
  • Proof snippets for follow-up
  • Clear handoff from interest to meeting
Why this is workable now

The ingredients already exist

1. Category story

Proteus is not generic equipment. It is a differentiated way to measure and train real-world movement through 3D resistance, performance data, and shareable insights.

2. Proof object

The assessment report appears central to the product story. That matters because it can do double duty as user value and as a conversion asset in the funnel.

3. Commercial story

The Qwilr page makes the business case explicit: monetized assessments, retention, pricing power, and a stronger facility offer. That is a real GTM angle, not a supporting detail.

0-30 day plan

What I would do first

Days 0-5 · choose the wedge and map the funnel

  • Choose the primary wedge: sports performance, rehab, or facility ROI
  • Review current site, sales flow, and existing proof assets
  • Map the current path from first touch to booked conversation
  • Identify where prospects get confused, lose confidence, or stall

Days 6-10 · package one flagship proof asset

  • Build one proof-led story around a customer, operator, or athlete outcome
  • Pull in the assessment report, quote, and commercial value narrative
  • Create short proof snippets for email, social, and sales follow-up
  • Turn one strong source into reusable funnel assets

Days 11-20 · tighten the conversion path

  • Ship one focused landing path for the chosen wedge
  • Clarify the CTA: assessment, demo, or strategy conversation
  • Use the report and proof assets to pre-sell the conversation
  • Align sales follow-up with the same segment story and proof stack

Days 21-30 · launch, learn, and sharpen

  • Publish a short founder-led sequence tied to the same wedge
  • Track which claims and proof points earn the strongest response
  • Refine the page, proof pack, and follow-up based on live feedback
  • Decide whether to deepen the first wedge or expand to the next one
What this should produce

Visible outputs by day 30

Core deliverables

  • One clear wedge-specific message
  • One flagship proof asset
  • One tighter landing path with a clear CTA
  • One short founder-led content sequence
  • One practical set of website and funnel recommendations

Metrics I would care about early

  • More qualified conversations from the target wedge
  • Higher confidence in sales calls because the story is clearer earlier
  • Better reuse of proof across site, email, and content
  • Clearer signal on which wedge and which promise convert best
What I need from the Proteus team

Proof inputs

  • Founder interviews or source material worth atomizing
