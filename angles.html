How it was created

The atomization logic behind the new angles

Source used: Proteus case study “Identifying Trainable Weakness & Justify Quality of Services,” centered on TRAIN PMT Southlake and a measurable 7 mph exit velo improvement.

Step 1 · Extract the strongest proof

  • Traditional lifts build force in one angle
  • Baseball performance depends on diagonal and rotational planes
  • Proteus training in sport-specific planes led to a measurable result
  • The hard proof point: 90 mph exit velo with a 7 mph improvement

Step 2 · Find the tensions inside the story

  • Linear training vs rotational sport demands
  • Generic programming vs diagnosis of the actual weakness
  • Athlete result vs business proof for the facility
  • Performance improvement vs premium service justification

Step 3 · Turn one case into multiple narratives

  • Coaching angle: stop training baseball in straight lines
  • Commercial angle: measurable proof justifies premium service
  • Assessment angle: find the weakness before prescribing the fix

Step 4 · Adapt for platform behavior

  • LinkedIn: depth, saves, and expertise
  • X: conversation, replies, and thread depth
  • Instagram: saves, shares, and clear takeaway value
  • Keep each post native instead of copy-pasting the same format everywhere
Angle options

Three ways to package the same proof

Option 1

Stop training baseball in straight lines

This angle reframes the case as a coaching insight: most training is still too linear for a rotational sport.

  • Best for coaches and performance audiences
  • Sharp, contrarian, easy to discuss
  • Connects programming choices to real-world outcome

Option 2

Measurable proof justifies premium service

This angle turns the athlete result into a business story: proof makes the facility easier to trust, price, and retain around.

  • Best for facilities, operators, and premium positioning
  • Bridges athlete result with GTM value
  • Strongest funnel-growth narrative

Option 3

Find the weakness before prescribing the fix

This angle makes the assessment logic the hero: better programming starts with correctly identifying where performance is being lost.

  • Best for assessment-first and rehab-adjacent storytelling
  • Makes the coaching logic feel intelligent and credible
  • Easy to expand into educational content
